Throughout my participation in the **First Cloud AI Journey** program, I had the opportunity to learn practical cloud computing knowledge, collaborate with teammates, and gain hands-on experience in developing and deploying an AWS-based project. The program provided valuable exposure to real-world cloud technologies while helping me improve both my technical and soft skills.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Learning Environment**  
The learning environment was well organized and supportive. The learning materials were structured logically, making it easier to follow the roadmap from fundamental AWS concepts to deploying a complete cloud application. The collaborative atmosphere also encouraged participants to share knowledge and support one another when facing technical challenges.

**2. Support from Mentor**  
The mentor was enthusiastic and always willing to answer questions whenever difficulties arose. Instead of simply providing solutions, the mentor encouraged independent thinking and guided us toward finding solutions ourselves. This approach helped me better understand AWS services and strengthened my problem-solving skills.

**3. Relevance to My Academic Background**  
The program closely matched my background in Information Technology and allowed me to apply knowledge learned at university to a real cloud project. At the same time, I gained practical experience with many AWS services that I had previously only studied theoretically.

**4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities**  
The workshop helped me gain hands-on experience with AWS services such as Amazon EC2, Amazon S3, Amazon RDS, Amazon VPC, AWS Lambda, Amazon CloudWatch, and Route 53. Besides improving my cloud computing knowledge, I also developed skills in cloud deployment, Git/GitHub collaboration, technical documentation, troubleshooting, and teamwork.

**5. Teamwork Experience**  
Working with teammates throughout the project helped me improve my communication and collaboration skills. We regularly discussed implementation approaches, divided tasks effectively, and supported one another when solving technical issues. This experience gave me a better understanding of collaborative software development.

**6. Overall Program Experience**  
Overall, the First Cloud AI Journey program provided a meaningful learning experience. The balance between theory and hands-on practice enabled me to understand cloud computing concepts more deeply while gaining confidence in deploying real applications on AWS.

---

### Additional Questions

**What did you find most satisfying during the program?**

The most satisfying part was successfully deploying the **Tracker Maintenance** project on AWS. Seeing the application run successfully after configuring multiple AWS services gave me a strong sense of accomplishment and significantly increased my confidence in working with cloud technologies.

**What do you think could be improved for future participants?**

It would be helpful to provide additional advanced workshops or optional technical sessions on topics such as AWS security best practices, CI/CD pipelines, containerization, and infrastructure automation. More opportunities for participants to present their projects and receive feedback would also enhance the learning experience.

**Would you recommend this program to your friends? Why?**

Yes. I would recommend the First Cloud AI Journey program because it offers practical, project-based learning, experienced mentors, and valuable hands-on experience with AWS services. It is a great opportunity for students who want to strengthen their cloud computing knowledge before entering the industry.

---

### Suggestions and Expectations

* Continue updating the learning materials to include newer AWS services and current cloud development practices.
* Organize additional technical sharing sessions where participants can present their projects and exchange experiences.
* Include more real-world case studies to help learners understand how AWS solutions are applied in industry.
* I would be interested in participating in future programs or advanced cloud workshops organized by First Cloud AI Journey.

Overall, I am grateful for the opportunity to participate in this program. It has strengthened both my technical knowledge and practical experience, providing a solid foundation for pursuing a career in cloud computing and software engineering.
