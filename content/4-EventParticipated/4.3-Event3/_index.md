# Event Summary: FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week: Show Up. Build. Pitch. WIN!

### Event Objectives

- Showcase innovative AI solutions developed during the Agentic AI Build Week Hackathon.
- Introduce modern AI technologies and AWS services used in real-world applications.
- Inspire participants to develop practical AI products through teamwork and innovation.

### Main Topics

#### Introduction to the Hackathon

- Overview of the Agentic AI Build Week organized by JIC Fund and AWS.
- Sharing from AWS Solution Architects about AI development and cloud technologies.
- Encouragement for participants to build practical AI solutions within a limited time.

#### Outstanding Project Demonstrations

Several teams presented innovative AI applications, including:

- **KFC Force Agent** – An AI-powered conversational food ordering system using Amazon Bedrock, DynamoDB, S3, AWS WAF, and Agent Core.
- **Signal Scout** – A multi-agent platform that analyzes competitors using React, AWS Amplify, Cognito, WAF, and intelligent AI agents.
- **SA Professional AI-Native Assistant** – An AI assistant capable of generating AWS architectures, estimating cloud costs, and creating Infrastructure as Code automatically.
- **S.H.E.P.H.E.R.D** – A smart crowd monitoring system using Amazon Kinesis Video Streams, ECS Fargate, YOLO object detection, and Amazon Bedrock.

#### Hackathon Lessons

- Focus on solving a clear problem with a practical MVP.
- Demonstrate a working product instead of only presenting ideas.
- Divide responsibilities effectively within the team.
- Communicate clearly and collaborate under time pressure.

### Knowledge & Skills Gained

- Learned how AI services can be integrated with AWS cloud infrastructure.
- Gained a better understanding of multi-agent systems and AI application architecture.
- Learned practical approaches for planning and developing an MVP during a hackathon.
- Understood the importance of teamwork, presentation, and technical communication.

### Applying to My Learning

The event expanded my understanding of how AWS services can support modern AI applications. The project demonstrations also gave me new ideas about combining cloud computing with artificial intelligence in future software projects.

### Personal Experience

This was one of the most inspiring events I attended during the program. Seeing real projects built within a short period of time demonstrated the importance of teamwork, creativity, and execution. The event also motivated me to continue learning AI technologies alongside cloud computing.
